After selecting Set reference line, move the mouse over the image and click to place the line. Keyboard alternative: Up and Down arrow keys (Shift for steps of 10 pixels), then Enter. (Evaluation and Reproducibility require the mouse.)

Keyboard positioning is fine for practice here, but the Evaluation and Reproducibility modules assess mouse positioning, as on the scanner workstation. Lines placed with the keyboard there do not count. Practice with the mouse to prepare for certification.
